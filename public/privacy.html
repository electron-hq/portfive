Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

At PortFive, accessible from portfive.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by PortFive and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, we collect the following personal information:

  • Account Data: Your full name, username, and securely hashed password.
  • Content Data: Images, videos (YouTube links), articles (Pens), comments, and messages you share on the platform.
  • Profile Data: Profile pictures, cover photos, bio, and website links you choose to add.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our platform.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our platform.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our platform.
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality.
  • Communicate with you for customer service, updates, and marketing purposes (if opted-in).
  • Track ad impressions for creator monetization (if you have "Allow Ads" enabled).

3. Third-Party Services

PortFive utilizes third-party services to ensure a fast and reliable experience. These third parties may process your data according to their own privacy policies:

  • MongoDB Atlas: For secure database hosting.
  • Cloudinary / ImgBB: For hosting and serving images and media.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: For CAPTCHA verification to prevent spam and bots.
  • Adsterra: For displaying advertisements (only if a creator enables the "Allow Ads" feature). Adsterra may use cookies to serve relevant ads.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, PortFive uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. We use HttpOnly cookies to securely store your authentication token (JWT). You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options, but this will log you out of your PortFive account.

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. You can do this directly from your Profile Settings by clicking "Delete Account". This will permanently remove your account, posts, messages, and associated data from our active databases.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at electron.team@proton.me.
