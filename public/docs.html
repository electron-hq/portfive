2. Creator Monetization

PortFive empowers creators to earn revenue directly from their visual content. Our ad system is designed to be non-intrusive and respectful of the viewer's experience.

How to Enable Ads

  • Navigate to your Profile Settings.
  • Toggle the "Allow Ads" switch to ON.
  • Once enabled, a sponsored banner (320x50) will automatically appear at the bottom of your Image posts in the full-screen scroller.

Where do the ads appear?

Ads are strictly limited to the Full-Screen Image Scroller. They appear as a small, elegant banner at the bottom of the screen, positioned above your profile name and caption.

What are the requirements for Monetized Posts?

  • When uploading an image for a monetized post, use the size 1080 x 2200 px.
  • Leave the bottom 280 pixels of your image visually clear. The ad banner and text overlay will occupy this space, ensuring your actual artwork remains unobstructed.
  • PortFive automatically tracks impressions for your profile. This data will be used to calculate your revenue share in future updates.

3. Copyright & Content Policy

PortFive is a platform built for original creators. We support YouTube embeds for video content, but strict rules apply to protect intellectual property:

  • You must be the original owner of the YouTube channel you are embedding.
  • Do not embed videos that you do not own or have explicit permission to distribute.
  • PortFive and the Electron Team are not liable for any copyright strikes or DMCA claims resulting from user-generated content.
  • Content found violating copyright laws will be removed immediately upon a valid request from the original owner.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

Is PortFive free to use?

Yes, PortFive is completely free for all users. We generate revenue through optional creator ads and sponsored placements, keeping the core experience free.

How do I become a Verified Creator?

To receive the official Verified Badge, you need to reach 100 followers. Once you hit this milestone, the badge is automatically applied to your profile.

Can I upload heavy video files directly?

No. PortFive is designed to be fast and lightweight. We currently support YouTube embeds for video content. Direct video uploads may be introduced in future updates.
