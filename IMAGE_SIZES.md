# PortFive Image & Media Guidelines

To ensure the best visual experience and fast loading times across the platform, please follow these image size guidelines.

## 1. Image Scroller (Standard Users)
*   **Recommended Size:** **1080 x 1920 pixels** (Aspect Ratio 9:16)
*   **Format:** JPG, PNG, or WebP
*   **Max File Size:** 2 MB
*   *Note:* This will fill the mobile screen perfectly.

---

## 💰 2. Image Scroller (For Monetized Creators with "Allow Ads" ON)

If you have turned on **"Allow Ads"** in your settings, your image will have a **320x50 Ad Banner** and a **User Overlay (Name & Caption)** at the bottom. 

*   **Recommended Size:** **1080 x 2200 pixels** (Aspect Ratio approx 1:2)
*   **Why?** The bottom **280 pixels** will be covered by the ad banner and your name/caption. By uploading a slightly taller image, your main photo will remain fully visible above the ad.
*   **If you upload 1080x1920 instead:** The system will automatically crop/resize it, but the ad and your caption might cover the bottom part of your image.

---

## 3. Pen (Article) Cover Image
*   **Recommended Size:** **1200 x 400 pixels** (Aspect Ratio 3:1)
*   **Format:** JPG, PNG, or WebP

## 4. Profile & Homepage Grid
*   **Profile Pic:** 400 x 400 pixels (1:1 Square)
*   **Homepage Grid:** 1080 x 1080 pixels (1:1 Square)

---

## ✂️ Auto-Cropping & Resizing Rule
PortFive uses smart auto-cropping. If you upload an image with different dimensions, the system will automatically crop it to fit the screen. 
*   For standard posts: It will crop to 9:16.
*   For ad-enabled posts: It will ensure the ad banner fits at the bottom. 
*   **Tip:** Always keep your main subject in the center of the image.
